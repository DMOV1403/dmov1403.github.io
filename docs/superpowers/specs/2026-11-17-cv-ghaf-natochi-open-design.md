# CV page: ghaf scene with natochi-style section opening

Short design (small change). Approved by Osama on 2026-11-17.

## Intent
Osama preferred the first version of `/cv/` (ghaf tree on moonlit dunes) over the leafy natochi-style rebuild,
but wants sections to open the way natochi.cv does.

## Goals
- Restore the ghaf scene: stippled ghaf tree growing on scroll, two dunes, crescent moon, stars,
  name and role on the first screen, handwritten branch labels ("me", "work", "the path", "say hi") with stippled leader lines.
- Open sections like natochi: tree shrinks and steps left, roots appear in a dark cut-away of the dune,
  section text in the middle column, nothing on the right.
- Close with "back to the tree", Esc, or the browser back button. Phones: text over the dimmed tree.

## Non-goals / anti-goals
- No video material (no collage, no player, no video link).
- No dark frame or grass from the leafy rebuild.
- Must not regress: labels stay real buttons (taps work in embedded phone viewers),
  faded intro must not catch taps, plain readable CV if the canvas fails, reduced-motion shows the grown tree.

## Approaches
1. **Restore the ghaf version and add the opening (chosen).** Keeps the scene Osama liked exactly.
2. Restyle the leafy build into a ghaf scene. Rejected: rebuilds the part he already liked.
3. Leave the scene and only change copy. Rejected: doesn't address the request.

## Decided
- Text: ghaf-version wording and type (Spectral, sentence case, taped number notes in "work"). Builder's call, open to change.

## Left to the builder
- Exact shrink scale and left position of the tree; root drawing details.
