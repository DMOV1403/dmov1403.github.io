the brief

An Arabic-speaking author wanted one home for everything he does: his books, his articles, his media, a way to sell books online, and a members’ library where readers pay once and read his books right in the browser, with a community growing around it. I built the whole thing, put it online under his own domain, and still look after it today.

what i built

Online book store

A public catalogue of his books, plus digital copies for sale. Physical orders work with cash on delivery or bank transfer, and every order moves through a clear workflow from pending to shipped.

Lifetime membership

Readers pay once for lifetime access to the premium library, in dollars through PayPal or in Egyptian pounds through Vodafone Cash.

In-browser book reader

Members read protected PDF books inside the site. Files are streamed securely, access is checked on every request, and downloads stay under control.

Community feed

Members post, react and hold threaded discussions, all moderated from the admin panel.

Notifications

An in-app bell for announcements, new book alerts and welcome messages, plus email reminders that send themselves in the background.

Admin panel

The author runs orders, books, users, payment approvals, subscriptions and announcements himself, without a developer for the day-to-day.

Accounts and email

Registration, login, email verification and password reset, with every email written in Arabic.

Articles and media

A space for his articles, next to an audio library, his biography, his services and what readers say about him.

payments and security

Four ways to pay under one roof: PayPal checkout with server-side capture and verified webhooks, a Vodafone Cash flow with admin approval for readers in Egypt, and cash on delivery or bank transfer for physical orders. Every payment is validated, logged and easy to audit.

It’s hardened like a commercial product: strict security headers, rate limiting on every sensitive action, hashed passwords, hardened sessions and verified webhooks. Access is re-checked against the database on every request, so if a payment is refunded or cancelled, access ends at once.

An editorial design system
