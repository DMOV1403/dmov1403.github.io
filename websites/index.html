Dr. Monir Lotfy — Digital Publishing Platform

Author platform · Online store · Membership library

The Brief

An Arabic-speaking author wanted one home for everything he does: his books, his articles, his media, a way to sell books online, and a members' library where readers pay once and read his books right in the browser, with a community growing around it. We built the whole thing, put it online under his own domain, and still look after it today.

  • Live atmonirlotfy.com
  • TypeFull-stack web application
  • LanguageArabic-first, fully RTL
  • LaunchHosting, domain & deployment handled
  • Scale22 pages · 15 database tables · 12 APIs

What We Built

◈

Online Book Store

A public catalog of his published books, plus digital copies for sale. Physical orders work with cash on delivery or bank transfer, and every order moves through a clear workflow from pending to shipped.

◈

Lifetime Membership

Readers pay once and get lifetime access to the premium library. They can pay in dollars through PayPal, or in Egyptian pounds through Vodafone Cash.

◈

In-Browser Book Reader

Members read protected PDF books right inside the site. Files are streamed securely, access is checked on every request, and downloads stay under control.

◈

Community Feed

Members post, react with emojis and hold threaded discussions, all moderated from the admin panel.

◈

Notification Center

An in-app bell for announcements, new book alerts and welcome messages, plus email reminders that send themselves in the background.

◈

Full Admin Panel

The author runs everything himself: orders, books, users, payment approvals, subscriptions and announcements. He does not need a developer for the day-to-day.

◈

Accounts & Email Flows

Registration, login, email verification and password reset, with every email written in Arabic.

◈

Articles & Media

A publishing space for his articles, next to an audio library, his biography, his services and what readers say about him.

Payments that fit the audience

Four ways to pay under one roof: PayPal checkout with server-side capture and verified webhooks, a Vodafone Cash flow with admin approval for readers in Egypt, and cash on delivery or bank transfer for physical orders. Every payment is validated, logged and easy to audit.

Production-grade security

Hardened like a commercial product: strict security headers, rate limiting on every sensitive action, hashed passwords, hardened sessions and verified webhooks. Access is re-checked against the database on every request, so if a payment is refunded or cancelled, access ends instantly.

02

RSD — Corporate Identity Site

Brand website · Mining, investment & industrial technology · Oman

The Brief

RSD is an Omani venture company that connects natural resources with international investors. They needed a site that feels like an established firm, loads fast anywhere in the world, and shows up on Google. We designed it, built it, set up the hosting, connected the domain and handled the SEO, start to finish.

  • Live atrsd.services
  • TypeCorporate brand site
  • LaunchHosting, domain & SEO handled
  • ApproachZero-dependency, hand-crafted code
  • Scale5 pages · custom design system

What We Built

◈

Editorial Design System

A serif and sans identity on a warm ivory palette, built around the brand story of Rasad, the word Arab astronomers used for observation. No template involved.

◈

Five-Page Architecture

Home, About, What We Do, Projects and Contact, telling the company's story across mining, investment and industrial technology.

◈

Motion & Interaction

Scroll reveals, a scrolling keyword ticker, a filterable project grid and a sticky header. Everything calms down for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

◈

Performance by Design

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no build step. The whole production site is about 63 KB, so it loads instantly and nothing can break.

◈

Complete SEO Launch

Clean URLs, a sitemap, structured data, social sharing cards and branded WhatsApp previews. Then we verified the site with Google ourselves and submitted it for indexing.

◈

Hosting & Domain, Done

We set up the hosting, connected the rsd.services domain, and keep the whole site versioned on GitHub as a permanent backup.

  • Live and indexed: domain connected, and all 5 pages picked up by Google
  • Responsive: built mobile-first, with a full-screen menu and accessibility baked in
  • True to the brand: designed from the company's own logo, story and sectors
  • Refined in the open: five design iterations with the client before the final look
  • Owner-friendly: plain files the client can host anywhere, cheaply, forever
  • Fully handed off: launch checklist, Google Search Console access and a simple update routine
