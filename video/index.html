how i make one

  1. The hook

    One idea that earns the first two seconds: a problem, a surprise or a picture you can’t scroll past.

  2. The script

    Short lines written for the voice, with the captions timed to it word by word.

  3. The edit, in code

    Footage, captions, motion graphics and the end card are assembled by a script, so nothing is placed by hand twice.

  4. Versions

    A new hook, a new cut or a new language is a small change to the script, not a new edit. That makes testing cheap.
